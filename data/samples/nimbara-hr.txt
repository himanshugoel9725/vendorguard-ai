Security & Trust Overview

Nimbara HR

Reporting period: 2025.

Nimbara HR provides a cloud payroll and human-resources platform for mid-sized employers. It processes employee records and bank details on behalf of its customers.

Services: Cloud payroll processing; Employee records and leave management; Payslip delivery portal.

Hosting: Stratus Cloud, Frankfurt region.

Data handled: employee and customer contact records; bank account and payment details.

Key suppliers: Stratus Cloud (hosting); Mailwave (transactional email); Payrail Gateway (payment file transfer).

Independent reports held: SOC 2 Type II; ISO/IEC 27001:2022.Section 1: Governance

The sections below follow the order in which customers usually ask their questions.

A written security policy covers all staff, and management approval is renewed at each annual policy review.

Security roles and responsibilities are documented, and a named security officer reports to the executive team.

A risk assessment is performed every year and after major changes, and findings are tracked in a risk register with risk treatment plans.

Regular company meetings give every employee a chance to raise questions with the leadership.Section 2: Access

Accounts for new staff are created on their first day at the company.

Access to systems and customer data follows an access control policy based on least privilege and role-based access.

All workforce accounts use MFA through the company single sign-on provider, with strong authentication on administrative interfaces.

Privileged access is limited to a small group of approved staff, and administrator accounts are separate, logged and reviewed.

An access review takes place once a year, and deprovisioning is handled manually for some systems.

The helpdesk answers questions about laptops and accounts during office hours.Section 3: Data handling

Customers can export their own data from the portal at any time.

Disk encryption and KMS-managed keys protect all databases and file storage, in line with our encryption at rest standard.

All customer traffic uses encryption in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher, and HTTPS is enforced on every public endpoint.

Data retention periods are defined in the contract, and customer data is deleted within 30 days of termination using secure deletion.

The privacy programme meets GDPR requirements, and PII is processed only under a data processing agreement.

Backups are taken regularly and kept offsite, but restore tests are informal.

Reports and dashboards are generated from the stored data on request.Section 4: Operations

New features are announced to customers in a monthly newsletter.

Security-relevant events are sent to a central SIEM for logging, and audit logs are kept for twelve months under our log retention rule.

Vulnerability scanning runs weekly, and patching of critical vulnerabilities follows a remediation SLA of 14 days.

Security groups restrict traffic between environments, and a web application firewall and intrusion detection protect public endpoints.

Operational dashboards show how fast pages load for customers around the world.Section 5: Resilience

Customers can follow the service status on a public page.

A documented incident response plan defines roles and severity levels, and a tabletop exercise tests it every year.

The company will notify customers through an incident notification within 72 hours, and affected customers receive regular updates.

A formal business continuity and disaster recovery plan is not in place, and no RTO or RPO has been set.

Offices have fire exits, evacuation signs and first-aid kits on every floor.Section 6: Engineering

Engineers use modern programming languages and shared libraries.

Software follows a secure development lifecycle in which every change gets a code review and SAST runs in the pipeline.

A change advisory board approves releases, and peer-reviewed changes move through the deployment pipeline.

The engineering group meets every Monday to discuss the week.Section 7: Suppliers and assurance

Customers receive support in several languages.

A subprocessor list is kept, and vendor risk reviews are informal.

A current independent audit confirms the in-scope services, and the attestation report is available to customers on request.

Partner events are held twice a year to share product news.Section 8: People

The company employs people in several countries and offers flexible working.

Awareness training is given to new hires, and a phishing simulation is on the roadmap.

Employees can join interest groups such as running and chess clubs.
